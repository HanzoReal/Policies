Data Deletion Instructions

For the Meta apps "postclipp" and "Wasteddailyig". Last updated: 22 November 2026.

What we store about you

Nothing. These apps only publish posts to the developer's own Facebook Pages and Instagram account and read the total counts of views, likes and comments. They never see who you are, even if you liked or commented on a post.

How to ask us to delete data

  1. Send a message with the words "Delete my data" to the Facebook Page or Instagram account that published the content (or open an issue on this website's GitHub repository).
  2. We check for any data about you and delete it within 30 days, then reply to confirm.

Removing an app from your Facebook account

If you ever connected one of these apps to your own Facebook account, you can remove it yourself: Facebook > Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites > select the app > Remove.
