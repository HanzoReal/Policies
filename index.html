Privacy Policy

For the Meta apps "postclipp" and "Wasteddailyig". Last updated: 22 November 2026.

1. What these apps are

These apps are private tools used only by their developer. They publish short gaming videos and image posts to Facebook Pages and an Instagram professional account that the developer owns and manages, and they read how those posts perform. They are not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to them.

2. What data the apps access

Only through Meta's official Graph API, and only with the developer's own permission:

  • the developer's own Facebook Pages and Instagram account: name, ID and follower count;
  • the posts the apps publish: their IDs, links and total counts (views, likes, comments).

The apps do not access personal data of the people who view, like or comment on the posts (only the totals), messages, friend lists, ad accounts or anyone's personal profile.

3. How the data is used

Only to publish the posts and to show the developer how they perform. The data is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold or shared with anyone. Post performance numbers (which contain no personal data) may be processed by an AI service the developer uses to write performance reports.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens, post IDs and performance numbers are stored on the developer's own computer and database. Tokens are kept secret and never published. Data is kept only as long as it is needed to run the tool, and is deleted on request.

5. Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).

6. Deleting your data

See the data deletion instructions.

7. Contact

Send a message to the Facebook Page or Instagram account that published the content, or open an issue on this website's GitHub repository.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.
